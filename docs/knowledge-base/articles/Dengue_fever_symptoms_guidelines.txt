Dengue Fever — First Aid Guide

TOPIC: dengue, dengue fever, dengue bukhar, breakbone fever, mosquito fever

RECOGNIZING DENGUE FEVER:
- Sudden high fever (up to 41°C / 106°F) starting 4–10 days after a mosquito bite
- Severe headache behind the eyes
- Severe joint and muscle pain ("breakbone fever")
- Skin rash appearing 2–5 days after fever begins
- Nausea and vomiting

WHEN TO SEEK EMERGENCY CARE:
Seek hospital immediately if any warning sign appears:
- Belly pain or tenderness
- Vomiting 3 or more times in 24 hours
- Bleeding from nose or gums
- Blood in vomit or stool (black or red)
- Extreme fatigue, restlessness, or confusion
- Fever drops but condition worsens — this is the most dangerous period (days 3–7)
- Skin turns pale, cold, or shows bruising or red spots not fading with pressure
- Fever persists beyond 7 days

WHAT TO DO WHILE WAITING:
1. Rest completely.
2. Drink plenty of fluids — water, ORS, coconut water, or clear broth. At least 2–3 litres per day.
3. Use paracetamol (acetaminophen) only to reduce fever and pain.
4. Monitor temperature and symptoms every few hours.
5. Watch for warning signs listed above — if any appear, go to hospital immediately.

DO NOT:
- Do not give aspirin or ibuprofen — they increase bleeding risk and can make dengue much worse.
- Do not allow the patient to get up and move around more than necessary.
- Do not dismiss worsening symptoms — dengue can become severe within hours.

PREVENTION:
- Use mosquito repellent on all exposed skin.
- Wear long sleeves and long trousers, especially during dawn and dusk.
- Sleep under mosquito nets.
- Empty any standing water containers (pots, buckets, tyres) — dengue mosquitoes breed in still water.
- Keep water storage containers tightly covered.
