Cholera — Field Recognition and Treatment

RECOGNIZING CHOLERA:
- Sudden onset of profuse watery diarrhoea (described as "rice water" stools — pale, watery, no blood)
- Rapid dehydration: dry mouth, sunken eyes, no urine, skin pinch does not spring back
- Vomiting alongside diarrhoea
- Muscle cramps (from fluid and salt loss)
- Symptoms appear 12 hours to 5 days after drinking contaminated water

IMMEDIATE TREATMENT — ORS (ORAL REHYDRATION SOLUTION):
Most cholera cases can be treated with ORS alone.
1. If ORS packets are available: dissolve 1 packet in 1 litre of clean water. Give continuously.
2. If ORS is not available — make home ORS: 1 litre clean water + 6 teaspoons sugar + 1/2 teaspoon salt. Stir until dissolved.
3. Adults should drink 750 ml (3 cups) per hour while diarrhoea is active.
4. Children under 5: give small sips frequently — 5 ml every 1–2 minutes.
5. Continue ORS even if the patient is vomiting — give small amounts slowly.

WHEN TO GO TO HOSPITAL IMMEDIATELY:
- Patient cannot drink or keep fluids down
- Diarrhoea is more than 10 times per hour
- Signs of severe dehydration: no urine in 6+ hours, sunken eyes, cannot stand
- Patient is confused, very weak, or unconscious
- Child is severely lethargic or unable to cry
Severe cases need intravenous fluids — this cannot be done at home.

PREVENTING SPREAD:
- Wash hands with soap and water after any contact with the patient or their waste.
- All water used for drinking or cooking must be boiled or treated with chlorine tablets.
- Bury or cover all human waste immediately — do not leave near water sources.
- Disinfect any surface or clothing that contacted diarrhoea using bleach solution (1 part bleach to 9 parts water).
- Do not eat raw food, uncooked shellfish, or food prepared without clean water.
- Breastfeed infants — breast milk does not transmit cholera and protects the child.

DO NOT:
- Give antibiotics without medical advice — they are only needed for severe cases.
- Stop ORS if vomiting occurs — reduce the amount and give more frequently.
- Allow others to drink from the same water source until it is treated.
