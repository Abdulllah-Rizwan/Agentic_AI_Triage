Understanding Triage Levels — Patient Guide

TOPIC: triage, triage levels, RED triage, AMBER triage, GREEN triage, waiting for treatment

RECOGNIZING YOUR TRIAGE CATEGORY:
When you are assessed, you will be placed into one of three categories based on urgency.

RED — CRITICAL (Immediate):
You need life-saving care right now.
Conditions: difficulty breathing, uncontrolled bleeding, unconsciousness, severe crush injury, seizures, heart attack symptoms.
What happens: You will be moved to the emergency treatment area immediately.

AMBER / YELLOW — URGENT (Can wait briefly):
You have a serious condition that needs treatment within the hour but is not immediately life-threatening.
Conditions: fractures, deep wounds, high fever, moderate bleeding that is controlled, severe pain.
What happens: You will be assessed and treated as soon as a team member is available.

GREEN — MINOR (Walking wounded):
Your condition is stable. You can walk and do not have immediate life-threatening symptoms.
Conditions: minor cuts, mild fever, sprains, nausea without vomiting.
What happens: You will be directed to a separate area and will wait but will be seen.

WHEN TO SEEK EMERGENCY CARE:
Alert a health worker immediately — or ask someone to go on your behalf — if:
- Your condition is worsening after being triaged
- You are having difficulty breathing
- Bleeding resumes or worsens
- You lose consciousness or feel about to faint
- You develop new symptoms (chest pain, confusion, severe pain)
You have the right to request reassessment if your symptoms change.

WHAT TO DO WHILE WAITING:

RED category — while waiting for emergency team:
1. Stay lying down.
2. Keep someone with you to monitor breathing.
3. Apply pressure to any wounds.
4. Do not eat or drink — you may need surgery.

AMBER category — while waiting:
1. Stay in the waiting area assigned to you.
2. Immobilize injured limbs — do not walk on a possible fracture.
3. Keep the patient warm and calm.
4. Re-approach a health worker immediately if: breathing becomes difficult, bleeding resumes, or consciousness decreases.

GREEN category — while waiting:
1. Rest in the designated area.
2. Drink water if no abdominal injury.
3. Use basic first aid for minor wounds — clean and bandage.
4. Do not leave the assessment area without being seen.
5. If you feel worse, go directly to any health worker and say: "I was triaged GREEN but my condition is getting worse — I need to be reassessed."

DO NOT:
- Do not leave the medical facility without being seen, even if you feel better — your condition may change.
- Do not give a RED-category patient food or water before they are seen by a doctor.
- Do not try to move a RED-category patient unnecessarily.
