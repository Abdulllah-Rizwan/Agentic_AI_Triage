Diarrhoea and Dehydration — First Aid Guide

TOPIC: diarrhea, diarrhoea, loose motions, ulti dast, gastroenteritis, stomach bug, dehydration

RECOGNIZING DANGEROUS DEHYDRATION:
- No urine in 6+ hours, or dark yellow urine
- Sunken eyes or sunken fontanelle in infants
- Dry mouth and tongue
- Skin pinched on abdomen does not spring back within 2 seconds
- Child is very weak, limp, or unconscious
- Adult is confused, cannot stand, or has rapid weak pulse

WHEN TO SEEK EMERGENCY CARE:
Go to hospital or health worker immediately if:
- Blood in the stool (dysentery — needs antibiotics)
- Diarrhoea for more than 14 days
- Signs of severe dehydration (see above)
- Child cannot drink at all or is unconscious
- Patient has high fever (above 38.5°C) with diarrhoea
- Adult passing more than 10 stools per day
- Vomiting so severe that ORS cannot be kept down at all

WHAT TO DO WHILE WAITING:
1. If ORS packets available: dissolve 1 packet in 1 litre of clean water.
2. If no ORS packets: home solution = 1 litre clean water + 6 teaspoons sugar + 1/2 teaspoon salt.
3. Give continuously. Adults: 200–400 ml after each loose stool. Children: 50–100 ml after each stool.
4. Give small sips frequently if patient is vomiting — do not stop ORS.
5. Continue ORS until diarrhoea stops.
6. Continue feeding — do NOT stop food during diarrhoea. Give soft, easily digestible foods: khichri, rice, bread, banana, boiled potato.
7. Continue breastfeeding throughout the illness — do not stop.

FOR CHILDREN UNDER 5:
- Offer small amounts of ORS every few minutes.
- Give zinc tablets if available (20 mg/day for children over 6 months, 10 mg/day for infants) — zinc reduces duration of diarrhoea.
- After 4 hours of ORS with no improvement — take to health worker.

DO NOT:
- Do not stop ORS if the patient is vomiting — give less at a time, more frequently.
- Do not give fruit juice or fizzy drinks — they worsen diarrhoea.
- Do not give children under 12 anti-diarrhoeal medication without medical advice.
- Do not give aspirin to children under 16.
- Do not stop feeding during diarrhoea — starvation worsens recovery.

PREVENTION — STOP SPREAD:
- Wash hands with soap and water after using the toilet and before preparing food.
- Drink only boiled or chlorine-treated water.
- Do not eat food prepared with unclean water.
- Bury or cover all human waste — keep it away from water sources.
